## 1. C Programming

### Q1. Count Composite Numbers
Given an integer `n`, count how many composite numbers exist between 1 and `n`.

Example:

```text
Input: 15
Output: 8
```

### Q2. Generate a Mathematical Sequence
The sequence follows the rule:

`a(n) = 2 * a(n-1) + 1`

The first term is 1.

Write a C program to print the first `n` terms.

Expected output for `n = 6`:

```text
1 3 7 15 31 63
```

### Q3. Pair Sum is Prime
Given the array:

```c
int arr[] = {2, 4, 7, 9, 11};
```

Count the pairs whose sum is a prime number.

A pair must use two different indices.

Example: `(2, 9)` is a valid pair because their sum is 11.

## 2. Aptitude

### Q4. Catch-Up Problem
A bus leaves a town at 9:20 AM and travels at 48 km/h.

Another bus leaves the same town at 10:05 AM and travels in the same direction at 60 km/h.

At what time will the second bus catch the first bus?

### Q5. Average Speed
A person travels:

- 120 km at 40 km/h
- 120 km at 60 km/h

Find the average speed for the entire journey.

### Q6. Age Problem
Five years ago, a father was four times as old as his daughter.

Seven years from now, he will be twice her age.

Find their present ages.

### Q7. Population Growth
The population of Town A is 20,000 and increases by 10% every year.

The population of Town B is 24,200 and increases by 5% every year.

After how many years will their populations become equal?

## 3. Logical Reasoning

### Q8. Water Jug Puzzle
You have two empty jugs:

- 5-litre jug
- 9-litre jug

You have unlimited water.

Measure exactly 7 litres using only these jugs.

You can fill, empty, or transfer water between them.

There are no measurement markings.

## 4. Matrix and Mathematical Patterns

### Q9. Spiral Matrix
Consider a 5 × 5 matrix filled in clockwise spiral order, starting from 1.

Find:

1. The sum of the main diagonal.
2. The sum of the opposite diagonal.
3. The sum of both diagonals, counting the centre only once.

Try to derive a formula instead of writing the entire matrix first.
